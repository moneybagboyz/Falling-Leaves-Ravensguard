# Phase 4 fun check: combat anywhere and exploration

A 25-minute session (plan §6, "fun checks"). Play a normal new game (not Ironman for the first run, so F5 and F9 work), at 1x unless an item says otherwise. After each item write one line under it: what happened, whether it was fun, and the answer to **"did you understand why that happened?"**. A "no" is the most useful answer: say what you expected instead.

Before you start: note the date, the build (title screen, bottom left), the PC, and whether you played Phase 3's check. F8 opens the bug report at any time (it adds a screenshot; nothing is sent).

## 1. A duel (4 minutes)
At a town with a practice yard, ask the trainer for a bout (onboarding's yard), or take a road encounter with a single robber and choose **Fight**.
- Swing with the left mouse (hold to wind up), block or parry with the right mouse, dodge with Ctrl + a direction. Try one feint (right mouse early in your own wind-up).
- Look for: the wind-up you can read on your opponent, the moment a parry opens a riposte, stamina running down when you swing heavy, the hit-stop and the sound on a solid blow.
- After it, press F7: find the line that decided the fight and expand it (Enter) to see the armour layers.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why did your best blow land or fail? Did the log's layer trace make sense?

## 2. A skirmish (4 minutes)
A road encounter with a band of three or more (an ambush, or a demand you refuse). Check the odds on the Fight button before choosing.
- Fight with a companion if you have one. Try a called shot: T, pick a gap (an armpit or the face of an open helm) with the arrows, F to strike.
- Look for: whether the odds on the button matched what happened, enemies flanking you, someone breaking and running (morale), a man going down and not getting up.
- After it, open the after-fight report (Shift+F7): casualties, the worst wound, the cause chain of a death.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why did they run (or not)? Did the called shot go through the gap, and could you tell?

## 3. Watch mode (3 minutes)
Start another fight and press **F6** to hand the hero to the AI. Shift+F6 changes the stance (aggressive, balanced, defensive, protect, subdue, flee when hurt).
- Watch for 30 seconds in each of two stances. Then take control back: press F6 again, or just move (taking back on input is on by default).
- Look for: the "why" line on the watch HUD, a danger pause ("Take control?") when it turns bad, the stance changing what the hero does.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Did the hero do what the stance promised? Was the danger pause at the right moment?

## 4. A defeat, a capture and an escape (5 minutes)
Pick a fight you will lose (a large bandit band, or soldiers you provoke). Let yourself be knocked down, or yield from the Q menu.
- Read the defeat screen: what was taken, what was left, the wounds, what to do first.
- If captured: on the captivity screen, look at the guards, the bonds and the odds; work the bonds or search for a pick, try to slip away at night, or wait for a ransom. If a companion is free, try "Take control of …", walk them to the camp and press E.
- Save (F5) while held, load (F9), and check that you are still held with the same screen.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why were you robbed or taken rather than killed? Why did the escape work or fail? Did the ransom's amount and who paid it make sense?

## 5. An heir takes over (3 minutes)
Let the hero die: lose a fight to men who kill, or (new) take a bad wound in a won fight and do nothing while it bleeds. Note what the character screen says about your heirs first.
- On the succession screen, read the order (sibling, child, parent, spouse, a companion). Pick the heir.
- Look for: where the heir starts, what they carry (their own clothes; the old hero's things lie where he fell), the coin (less the funeral), the chronicle entry.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why this heir and not another? Did you know where the old hero's sword is?

## 6. A crypt crawl by torchlight (5 minutes)
Find a crypt or a barrow (the map's site icons; ruins and barrows near old battlefields). Light a torch (V) before going down; take a spare.
- Go down, follow the auto-map, open a niche or a bier, mind the traps (a pressure plate, a dart), fight what lives there. Watch the torch burn down and the light shrink.
- If you are hurt, read the HUD's surgeon line ("1.2 days on foot, 0.7 by horse"). Treat yourself from the Q menu (bandage), then get out (retrace the path from the Q menu).
- Look for: the dark beyond the torch, the sound of something moving, the moment you decide to turn back, the haul reaching your pack when you come up.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why did the trap go off? Why did the injury matter (or not), and what would a surgeon have done?

## 7. A mounted charge (3 minutes)
Mount a horse (the loaned horse or one you bought) and ride into a road fight, or charge a group at a gallop (Shift).
- Charge with a lance or a spear if you have one, else a sword from the saddle. Try once on a road and once on mud or a field.
- Look for: the charge's weight (the HUD's charge preview), a rider unhorsed, the horse shying or refusing, how much worse the charge is on mud.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why did the charge break them (or not)? Did the ground make the difference you expected?

## At the end (1 minute)
- Which of the seven was the most fun, and which the least?
- One thing you wanted to do in a fight and could not.
- Frame rate: did any fight or the crawl stutter? Note where.
- Anything that looked wrong (a doubled nameplate, text off the screen, a stuck screen). Use F8 for each.
