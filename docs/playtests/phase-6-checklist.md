# Phase 6 fun check: society, factions, rule, faith and family

A 30 minute session (plan §6, "fun checks"; overview §3 step 7). Play a normal new game (not Ironman for the first run, so F5 and F9 work), on a **new world** (so its history uses the Phase 6 rules; the world library row says "History rules: Phase 6"). Most items take game days: use 8x (key 4) when nothing hostile is near, and F5 before each item so you can go back. After each item write one line under it: what happened, whether it was fun, and the answer to **"did you understand why that happened?"**. A "no" is the most useful answer: say what you expected instead.

Before you start: note the date, the build (title screen, bottom left), the PC, and whether you played Phase 5's check. F8 opens the bug report at any time (it adds a screenshot; nothing is sent). **H** opens the Chronicle at any time: after each item, find the entry it wrote and follow its Why (F) back to the start. **F** opens the Faction screen, **L** the Ledger, **J** the Journal.

The review behind this check is `docs/reviews/phase-6.md`; its known limits say what is not in yet (guards do not chase you through the street yet, slaves fight only in road encounters, heists run in their own panel with the world clock held).

## 1. Get caught stealing and stand trial (6 minutes)
In a market town, stand by a stall with people around. Watch the HUD's eye: **Seen** (with a count) or **Unseen**. Take something from the stall while Seen (Q on the stall).
- Walk away. At the next morning look in the Journal for the Wanted card and the bounty, and in the Chronicle for the theft and its chain (witnessed, reported, bounty).
- Go back into town and give yourself up (Q > Give yourself up near a guard), or let the guards take you. In the gaol cell screen, read the charges and the sentence to expect before you choose a plea or a bribe.
- Wait for the court day (the last day of the five-day week, at 8:00) and the sentence the next morning.
- Look for: the witnesses named, the price of the bounty, the cell screen telling you what the law of this place does to a thief (stocks, flogging, a hand for a third theft), the sentence carried out through real wounds (look at the paper-doll and the treatment screen afterwards).
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Did the sentence match what the cell screen said? Did the Chronicle's Why read right?

## 2. Court and marry (5 minutes)
Find an unmarried person of an age to wed (talk window, Q > Talk). Call on them, give a gift or two over a few days, then make the plea.
- You need a roof with a free bed (a house you own or rent). If the answer is yes, the wedding waits for the next holy day; the priest's fee and the bride's dowry go through the Ledger.
- After the wedding open the Family panel (C > Family) and wait a season or two at 8x for a child.
- Look for: the courted one answering first, then the head of their house; the dowry arriving; the spouse joining your household; a child noticed in the Chronicle.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** If you were refused, did you know why (score, the head of the house, the church, no roof)?

## 3. Fight a street fire (5 minutes)
In a village with thatched houses, wait for a windy day (the weather line), then light a fire (a torch on thatch, or let a hearth accident happen: F5 first). Stand back and watch, then join the bucket chain (Q near the well) or pull down thatch with a hook.
- Look for: the fire running downwind, smoke, villagers forming a chain from the well, the stone church not catching, roofs falling in, the losses and any deaths in the Chronicle with the hearth (or you) as the cause.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why did it stop where it stopped (wind, rain, stone, the chain)?

## 4. Break a friend out (5 minutes)
Get a companion jailed (let them steal while seen, or use item 1's gaol). At night, Q near the gaol > slip into the gaol (or the drain if the town has one). In the heist panel: watch the guards' beats, the light and the ear line, pick the lock or take the sleeping gaoler's key, free your friend, leave.
- Do it once unseen (F5 first), then once letting a guard see you.
- Look for: unseen, the friend simply gone in the morning with no bounty; seen, bounties on both of you and a one-day manhunt. The Chronicle names what was seen.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Did you know why a guard heard or saw you?

## 5. Ride out a plague (4 minutes)
A great plague is rare (about one start a continent-year in a hundred). For this check use the world settings' plague slider on "frequent" in a test world, or wait for the news ("plague on the roads") and travel toward it.
- Watch a town's deaths day by day (the Character screen's "The times" row), the quarantine, a farmstead emptied, and afterwards the Ledger: wages up, land cheap.
- Nurse a sick member of your household (Character > Sickness > Nurse) or pay a physician.
- Look for: the plague following the trade roads, the dead with the plague as the cause, the scarcity terms easing season by season after it passes.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Did the prices after the plague make sense?

## 6. Rise to a manor (5 minutes)
Take contracts for a lord (J > the notice board: guard, escort, a bounty). Then press **F**: the Faction screen's Actions tab says what each step needs ("He will say no: what he thinks of you ... needs 40"). Swear fealty, ask for a manor, then a vacant fief; pay the relief.
- As lord: open the Ledger's Fief tab (receipts by source, arrears, grievances, each official's record). Appoint a reeve and a bailiff; raise the tax a level and watch grievances and the unrest rung. Hold court (Q > Hold court) on a day with a docket.
- Look for: the Actions tab telling you why a lord says no, the season's receipts, an official who skims found out by the audit, the unrest ladder moving with the tax.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Could you tell from the Fief tab why money came in or did not?

## After the session
- What was the most fun? What was confusing?
- Ask the testers (2 to 3) the same question for each item: **"did you understand why that happened?"**
- File anything broken with F8, and note the build.
