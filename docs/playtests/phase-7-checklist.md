# Phase 7 fun check: armies, war at scale, ships and industries

A 30 minute session (plan §6, "fun checks"). Play a normal new game, not Ironman, so F5 and F9 work. Use a **new world**, so the army, coin and industry content is in it.

- Most items take game days. Use 8x (key 4) when nothing hostile is near, and press F5 before each item so you can go back.
- After each item write one line under it: what happened, whether it was fun, and the answer to **"did you understand why that happened?"**. A "no" is the most useful answer; say what you expected instead.

Before you start, note the date, the build (title screen, bottom left), the PC, and whether you played Phase 6's check.

Keys for the whole session:
- **F8** opens the bug report.
- **H** is the Chronicle: follow each entry's Why (F) back to its start.
- **L** is the Ledger, with the new Industry tab and coin in gold, silver and copper.
- **F1–F4** select a formation in a fight, and **R** (hold) opens the order wheel.

The review behind this check is `docs/reviews/phase-7.md`. Its known limits say what is in the simulation but not on screen yet:
- the Army tab;
- the AI's sieges, sea raids and ship fights, which run but are mostly reported rather than shown live;
- the abstract war's lighter-than-planned toll, moved to Phase 8.

## 1. Pay in three coins (3 minutes)
Buy a meal, a tool and a horse. Look at prices and your purse in gold, silver and copper (1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp).
- Look for: small things in copper, wages in silver, land and horses in gold. Change given back correctly.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Did any price look wrong for what it is?

## 2. Run a works and find its bottleneck (6 minutes)
Buy or claim land at a clay pit or a woodland, and build the Level 1 plan of the first two stages (hands-on, as in Phase 5). Hire a crew.
- Open L > Industry. Read each stage's throughput, the highlighted bottleneck, the crew, the wages, and the balance sheet per day and per working week.
- Starve one stage: let the firewood or the barrels run out. Read why it stopped.
- Look for: raw output barely covering wages, finished goods paying better, and a plain reason when a stage stops.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Could you tell what to upgrade next, and why?

## 3. Sell up the road (4 minutes)
Sell part of the works' output at the gate to a passing merchant, then send the rest with your own small caravan to a town that lacks it. Hire a master and a guard.
- Look for: the expected margin from prices you know, the far town paying more, and its price falling as you flood it.
- **Did you understand why that happened?**

## 4. Lead your levy against a host on the road (8 minutes)
As a landholder in a realm at war, answer the levy call or call your own men. When a hostile host is reported on the road (the news, or the Chronicle), ride out with your men and meet it.
- In the fight, select formations with F1–F4. Hold R for the order wheel and try hold, shield wall, set against cavalry, and charge. Use the tactical pause to drag a formation and draw its facing.
- Watch a cavalry charge into braced spears, and one into men caught in the open.
- Look for:
  - formations holding a line;
  - a rout spreading;
  - riders refusing braced spears;
  - prisoners taken by rank;
  - the war score moving in the Chronicle;
  - the harvest you missed while levied.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Why did the line break where it broke?

## 5. Watch a siege (5 minutes)
Find a walled place under siege (the news, or a host camped by a wall), or build a stone wall on your own land and wait for a war to reach it (F5 first).
- Stay within 3 km when the walls are stormed, so the assault is fought live. Farther away it is reported.
- Look for:
  - engines built in the camp from timber, rope and stone the host bought;
  - stones hitting the near wall;
  - the breach after days, not hours;
  - the town's food running down;
  - a surrender or a storm, and the ransom.
- **Did you understand why that happened?** Did the time to breach feel right?

## 6. Hire a company and fail to pay it (4 minutes)
Hire a mercenary company (the contracts board in a town at war). Pay it for a week, then let its wages fall behind.
- Look for:
  - the advance going into escrow;
  - the weekly pay;
  - grumbling after a day or two of arrears;
  - the company leaving after six days, and plundering nearby if its name is bad.
- **Did you understand why that happened?**

## 7. Meet a sea raid (optional, coastal start only)
On a coast near a raider culture, wait for a raid at 8x (F5 first). The bell rings and the fief's men are sent.
- In this build the beach fight is reported, not fought live. Read the Chronicle: the ships, the plunder, the captives, and whether the raiders were caught.
- **Did you understand why that happened?**

## After the session
Write three lines:
- the best moment;
- the most confusing moment;
- the one thing you would change first.
