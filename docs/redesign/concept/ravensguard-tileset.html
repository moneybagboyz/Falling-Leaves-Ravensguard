Ravensguard Tileset

A first pass at the game's look in the spirit of Cataclysm's UltiCa tileset. Everything here is original medieval art I drew as code: 32×32 tiles where one tile is one metre, 32×64 layered characters, 16×16 items, one fixed palette. Scenes are shown at native size; sheets are zoomed in whole steps.

items · 4×

Items and the palette

  • One palette. About 48 colours, shared by every sprite. Faction colours and metals are palette swaps, not redraws.
  • Drawn as code. Each sprite is a small program: shapes, patterns and a light-from-top-left shading rule, then an automatic dark outline.
  • Grows by parts. A new armour is a few drawn parts; the tool bakes every direction and animation frame from the rig.
  • Honest note. This is plainer than UltiCa, which is the work of many artists over years. If it's not good enough, the fallback is to commission the character sprites and keep these tiles.

Everything on this page is generated in your browser from sprite code, the same way the game's art tool would bake its atlases. Style sheet v1: tell me what to change.
