## Done in the close-out (step 8, from `phase-4-open.md`)
Decided with the user (Nov 2026):
1. **The spear** is back at 0.40 / 0.50 s and countered: a parried, slipped or beaten-aside point puts the swordsman inside its reach, and a crowded polearm is choked up and steps back. It wins 62% against an unarmoured and 59% against a padded swordsman (the ≥ 55% floor of resolver-v2 §8; the physics gives the rest).
2. **The weapon × armour matrix** (`-- --matrix`, `docs/calibration/weapon-armour-matrix.md`): four expectations broke (club, staff and blunt arrow bounced off cloth; a dagger's point crushed plate; arrows went through plate; the maul did nothing through plate) and were fixed by three physics rules (rigid-only deflection, a failed edge skids off plate, a backplate is not met from the front) and two data changes (the maul's velocity, iron bodkin and quarrel heads). `WeaponArmourTests` pins the directions.
3. **Ranged realism runs** (`-- --ranged`, 15 pairings in the melee-and-ranged Notes): archers beat a lone swordsman unless he wears plate, a crossbow wins 55% on its one spanned shot, javelins 82 to 88%; two AI faults fixed (the archer nocks as he backs off, the crossbowman stands to span).
4. **Blunt weapons damage rigid armour:** heavy blunt blows dent and buckle plate (150 pm at the crush need; protection falls with condition), heavy blows burst mail and slip a battered plate's straps, told in the log and drawn on the doll (a mace takes a breastplate under 300 in about five blows, a sword scuffs 3 pm a blow).
