# Phase 6 code and bug check

Reviewed at db10bd7 (every Phase 6 step merged: society, factions, rule, faith and family): the code added or changed since b5d9234 closed Phase 5 (`git diff --stat b5d9234..db10bd7`: 592 files, about 53,600 lines added). Confidence is given per finding: **high** means read and traced end to end, or tested; **medium** means the mechanism was read but how often it happens in play was not measured.

The open list (`docs/reviews/phase-6-open.md`) was read first. Items it already records as "not built" are not repeated here, unless the gap breaks a part that *is* built (S4, S6, S11).

### Notes
- **The hero's id 0** (S6, S7, S8) still causes bugs, as the open list warned (Phase 4 S13, the A3 note). Other modules handle it correctly: bondage (`Slavery.Nobody`), D2 (`Opinions.K`), crime (`-1` for an unknown perpetrator), `Road` (`IsHero`). The failures are where `EntityId.None` or `0` is read as "nobody". A grep of the new modules for `== EntityId.None`, `!= EntityId.None` and `== 0` on person ids found these three. The others were checked and are places, entries or deliberate. A non-zero hero id in a later save step would remove the whole class of bug.
- **S1 and S15** are the Phase 5 S1 lesson again: a test of "resume" has to rebuild the world the way a real load does.
- **S2 and S3** together mean that a kin kidnapping ends in a free release after two days, and a rescue contract posted for it pays out.

## Checked and found clean
- **Determinism:** no `float`, `MathF`, `Math.Sin`/`Pow`/`Exp`, `DateTime`, `Stopwatch`, unseeded `Random` or `Parallel` in the new sim code; the doubles are listed in S16. The books are `SortedDictionary`/`SortedSet` or lists in id order. The hash sets in the new modules are used for lookup only. Two plain `Dictionary` iterations feed results, `FaithEngine.Season`'s `taken` (the bishops' stipends) and `FireWorld.HourStep`'s `boxes`. Both are filled once in key order with no removals, so they iterate in insertion order; this is deterministic in practice but rests on an implementation detail. Every draw is keyed on ids and game time. Stream and draw-index pairs within each new module do not collide (Diplomacy 0x671 draws 0 to 4, Relics 0x656 draws 0 to 2 on separate key spaces, Bondage 0x631 draws 1000 and up against 100 and up, 0x632 draws 0 to 2). The live hash trail is unchanged (Phase 5 S1 respected); the Phase 6 hashes join `phase5_trail` only once non-empty.
- **Conservation:** every coin path in the new modules found by grep goes through `Ledger.Transfer`. That covers fines, bounties (job escrow), dowries, wedding and marriage fees, dues, relief, wardship, aids, subsidies, tallage, customs, loans (principal in and out by transfer; a default keeps the loss), skims, bribes, tithes and the see's split, alms, penance, relic sales, pardoners, riot loot, the dole, ransoms (lord and kin), contract escrow, bonds and refunds, tournament purses, physician fees (balance checked first), slave sales, the peculium and self-purchase, the heist chest, NPC land sales and the hero's offer. The only exceptions are S10 (`Burn`) and S14 (an abstract delta). No `Mint` was added. New people come only through the population paths (children by `FamilySystem` and births; foals are animal rows), and every death goes through `Demography.Kill` or the wound book with a cause.
- **Saves:** no version bump. Every new section is written only when not empty (`IsEmpty`/`IsBlank`; difficulty only off its default), and each Phase 6 key is additive (`ChronicleEntryData` 6 and 7, `CauseData` 4 and 5, `WoundSave` 32 and 33). Each book's fields were compared with its save class (rule, politics, unrest, family, contracts, bondage, justice, crime, church, faith politics, heists, holdings, disease, standing, fire, landworks). Every stored field is captured; the fields not captured are derived (settings, kinds, hooks, `Near`, `NowDay` reset by the tick). `HistorySettings.RulesVersion` defaults to 0, so library worlds stay on v0. Enums were checked by diff: `CauseKind`, `TransferReason`, `Occupation`, `DeathCause`, `CaptorKind`, `WinnerKind`, `ActionKind`, `OddJobKind`, `RumourKind`, `LedgerTab`, `FollowerOrigin`, `BookEventKind`, `CasusBelliKind` and `HeroCommandKind` are all appended (`HeroCommandKind`'s order within Phase 6 differs from the step notes, which matters only to traces recorded mid-phase). The `ContractTests` transfer-reason test the E4 note says failed now passes.
- **Integration seams checked:** `Courting.ChurchAllows` is the single wedding check (courtship, plea, arranged match), and `RefusesWedding` is not called. `LawWorld.Grievance` → `UnrestConcede.KinHarsh` is wired. The Phase 2 riot and revolt are suppressed when E1 is attached (`RiotsElsewhere`; evasion is S13). Eviction without guards completes at once (`GuardsEnforce` is null, as the open list says). The D1 slave rising is raised once, through `UnrestWiring.SlaveRising`. Systems: each new `[AbstractSystem]` is a no-op without its book, and no system is registered twice; `StartRuntime` and `AttachTalk` run once per street view, and a street view gets a runtime only from a new game, a load or a replay. Ordering: faith at S4 + 10, then rule at S4 + 20, then politics at S10; unrest after the tax (`DailyGlobalsTaxes + 10`); disease after the population.
- **Swallowed exceptions:** none new (one `catch (KeyNotFoundException)` returns null for an unknown body plan).
