# Phase 8 economy and growth audit (P8-EA-1)

Audited commit: 2b78e0b (branch `claude/inspiring-galileo-gn2lby`, "the lighter testing regime"), worked on branch `p8-ea-1`. Brief: overview 2.6, the P8-EA-1 row, and the user's decisions of 28 Nov (population stable within plus or minus 10 percent over a century without famines rising and without hunger as the brake; resources renew where they really do, ore bodies are finite and prospectors find new ones; over-cutting makes timber and charcoal dear with the cause in the chronicle; D11: a gate the honest fix cannot meet ships as a named ratchet that may only tighten, and no existing bound is loosened). Method as `phase-7-audit.md`: red-team measurement first, findings ranked with evidence, then fixes, then the measurement again.

Severity as `economy-audit.md` 6: **High** a soak gate failed or a stated design rule does not hold; **Medium** a target missed or a consequence invisible; **Low** calibration, wording or a hook not wired.

## 1. What was run

- **The century soak**, seed 1234, 100 game years, every Phase 6 system attached (`Phase6SoakTests.Soak100Years_*`, `FL_SOAK100=1 FL_SOAK_YEARS=100`), **with the scripted player bot** (steal, surrender, court, burn, fight, as OV-5) **and without it** (`FL_SOAK_NOBOT=1`, new). The bot run had `FL_PF_STATS=1`. A game year took 13 to 20 s at the start and 90 to 106 s at year 100 (four runs sharing four cores).
- **The measurement hook** is `Sim/Diagnostics/EconomyCentury.cs` (read-only, never saved, never read by the sim): a yearly row of population, households, idle adults, hungry households and hunger deaths, food held a head, field fertility, fallow, cleared rows, the woodland's stock and standing cap, fish and game, the mines (open, working, worked out, closed) and their ore left, the ore mined in the year, the median mid prices over the books with a merchant of wheat, logs, planks, charcoal, bar iron and woollen cloth, the coin in purses and circulating, businesses open, closed, empty and failing (a loss season), and the brakes on births (co-resident wives of 18 to 44, those whose food reserve cuts conception, those crowded; unmarried adults and those waiting for a home; free dwellings; births, marriages, immigrants, emigrants). The soak prints the row each year (`[econ]`) and the decade table at the end (`EA-1 by decade`).
- **Probes** (`EconomyAudit/Ea1ProbeTests`, by hand with `FL_EA1_PROBE=1`): the veins and mine sites of seeds 1234, 1, 7, 42 and 99, and every mine's crew, box, store and book, year by year.
- **Before and after.** The "before" runs are the audited commit plus the hook only. The "after" runs (section 4) carry every fix of section 3.
